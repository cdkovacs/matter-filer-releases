Dropbox Matter Filer

Drag an email out of Outlook and file it into the right Dropbox matter folder as a PDF, with a dated entry in the matter's notes doc — reviewed and confirmed in your browser.

Download for macOS Universal (Apple Silicon & Intel) · all versions & release notes

Requirements

  • macOS 12 (Monterey) or later — Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • Microsoft Outlook and Google Chrome installed.
  • A Dropbox account with access to the firm's matter folders.

Install

  1. Download the installer above and double-click it. It's signed and notarized, so it opens without Gatekeeper warnings.
  2. Follow the installer. It sets up a background helper and a review screen at http://127.0.0.1:8765, and creates a Matter Filer folder in your home folder.
  3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 and click Connect Dropbox once to authorize your own account.

Everyday use

1. Drag
Drag an email from Outlook into Matter Filer › Inbox in your home folder.
2. Review
Open the review screen. The matter is pre-selected; adjust the name, summary, or matter if needed.
3. File
Click File selected. The PDF goes to the matter's Communications folder and a dated entry is appended to its notes doc.
New matter?
Pick ＋ New matter and browse your Dropbox to the matter's notes doc. Nothing files until you confirm.

The ⚙ Settings and 🗂 Matters ledger links in the review screen let you adjust preferences and edit the matter registry.

Notes

  • Your matters, the queue, and backups live in ~/Matter Filer/ and are never overwritten by an update.
  • Email content is summarized on-device; nothing is sent to a cloud AI service.
  • Updating is just installing a newer version — your data is preserved.
