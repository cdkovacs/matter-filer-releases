The review screen

Filing happens in one local screen at 127.0.0.1:8765. Each email arrives with its matter already picked and a filename built in the firm's convention. You glance, adjust anything that's off, and file.

New client, no matter yet

Create the matter without leaving the queue. Fill in the student, pick the matter's notes doc from your Dropbox, and click Add matter to ledger — it lands in the ledger and appears in every email's matter list at once, so a new client's whole batch files in one pass.

01

How a filing goes

DRAG

Out of Outlook

Drag the message into the Matter Filer › Inbox folder in your home folder.

REVIEW

Confirm the matter

Open the review screen. The matter is pre-selected; adjust the name, summary, or matter if it's wrong.

FILE

One click

Click File selected. The PDF lands in Communications and the dated line is added to the notes doc.

The history screen

The paper trail keeps itself. Every filed email is on the History screen — when it was filed and to which matter — with one-click links to the PDF and the notes entry it produced.

02

Install

  1. Download the installer and double-click it. It's signed and notarized, so it opens without Gatekeeper warnings.
  2. Follow the installer. It starts a background helper and a review screen at 127.0.0.1:8765, and creates a Matter Filer folder in your home folder.
  3. Open the review screen and click Connect Dropbox once to authorize your own account.
03

Requirements

  • macOS 12 (Monterey) or later — Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • Microsoft Outlook and Google Chrome installed.
  • A Dropbox account with access to the firm's matter folders.
04

Good to know

  • Your matters, the review queue, and backups live in ~/Matter Filer/ and are never overwritten by an update.
  • Email content is summarized on your Mac — nothing is sent to a cloud AI service.
  • Updating is just installing a newer version; your data carries over.
  • New matter? Browse your Dropbox to its notes doc — the app remembers it by a stable file ID, so renaming folders won't break filing.
